The first filter is the resume alone. No test, no call.

## What was reported

- Around **250 to 300 students** were shortlisted from the resume for the second round (the candidate's estimate).
- The candidate put **Machine Learning projects** and **Math-related coursework** on the resume.
- They were shortlisted **despite a low CPI**, and concluded GS reads resumes carefully. This is the candidate's inference, not something GS said.

## How to use this

- Lead with projects that show mathematical or ML depth, and list the maths coursework that backs them (probability, statistics, linear algebra).
- Pick projects you can be questioned on in detail. The interviews went into a Yelp dataset NLP project, scikit-learn usage and an internship project on a stock-market flash crash. See [Interviews](../03-interviews).
- Be ready to talk about a low CPI if you have one. Interviewers showed interest in it, and the advice was to prepare good and genuine reasons.
- If finance or markets appears under interests, it will be asked about. Only list what you actually follow.
