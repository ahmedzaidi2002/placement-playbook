A HackerRank test with **three sections of 45 minutes each**.

## Format and marking

| Question type | Marks | Negative marking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coding | +20 | none | Solved in the hosted compiler |
| Integer type (fill in the blank) | +10 | none | Answer up to 2 decimal places |
| MCQ | +10 | −3 | Some had four statements where you pick the correct ones |

You must do **well in any two of the three sections**, but a high overall score makes the interviews smoother, and **interviewing preference is set by test score**.

## The three sections

### Programming (Networking, Coding)

- 2 coding questions.
- Direct questions on the **time complexity of well-known algorithms**.
- Questions on **prefix and postfix expressions** (stack).
- Optimised code matters. A straightforward solution may time out on most test cases.

### Quant

- Basic aptitude: cattle grazing in a **circular field** while tied to a pole with a rope, and **dice-rolling probability**.
- **Expectation, covariance and correlation.**
- **Matrices and determinants.**

### Data Science

- ML theory, quite conceptual: for example, **what to do about missing data**.
- Some questions gave four statements and asked you to select the correct ones.

## What happens after

Around **60 students** were taken forward for interviews. The candidate's warning: many of those were selected "just in case", and some batchmates did not get a second interview after a first one went badly. A higher score also leaves you more energy to spend on the interviews.
