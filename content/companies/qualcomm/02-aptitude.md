### Q1 · Problem Solving

A point P lies on the line x + y = 8 and satisfies angle APB = 90 deg, where A(1,1) and B(7,5). If the x-coordinate of P is less than its y-coordinate, then P is:

**Options**

- **A.** (1, 7)
- **B.** (7, 1)
- **C.** (3, 5)
- **D.** (2, 6)

{{< details title="Show answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: D.** (2, 6)

Angle APB = 90 means P lies on the circle with AB as diameter: centre (4,3), r^2 = 13. Sub y = 8 - x into (x-4)^2 + (y-3)^2 = 13 gives x^2 - 9x + 14 = 0, so x = 2 or 7 → P = (2,6) or (7,1). Since x < y, P = (2,6).

### Q7 · Problem Solving

**Options**

- **A.** 16 : 23
- **B.** 23 : 17
- **C.** 18 : 23
- **D.** 17 : 23

{{< details title="Show answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: D.** 17 : 23

70 L → P = 30, Q = 40. Split 2:3 = 28 L and 42 L. Smaller 28 L: P=12, Q=16; +4 P → P=16, Q=16. Larger 42 L: P=18, Q=24; +6 Q → P=18, Q=30. Total P = 34, Q = 46 → 34:46 = 17:23.

### Q18 · Problem Solving

Car P leaves point O at 09:00 at 72 km/h. Car Q leaves O at 09:20 at 96 km/h. Car P makes a 15-minute refuel stop starting at 10:05. When does Q first overtake P, and at what distance from O?

{{< details title="Show answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

### Q19 · Problem Solving

A tray contains 5 standard components, 3 components needing rework, and 2 rejected components. Three components are inspected one after another without replacement. What is the probability that exactly one rejected component is inspected, and it is inspected before any component needing rework?

**Options**

- **A.** 3/10
- **B.** 11/30
- **C.** 37/120
- **D.** 5/16

{{< details title="Show answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: C.** 37/120

Total = 10 components: 5 standard (S), 3 rework (J), 2 rejected (R). Three are drawn without replacement. We want **exactly one R**, and it must come **before any J**. Three cases:

- R + 2 S: probability **20/120** (= 1/6)
- R, S, J with R before J: probability **15/120** (= 1/8)
- R first, then 2 J: probability **2/120** (= 1/60)

Sum = 20/120 + 15/120 + 2/120 = **37/120**.

*Confidence: medium. The answer depends on compiler behaviour or on reading an ambiguous question.*

### Q20 · Puzzles

Find the missing number. 3, 5, 4 → 29 ; 4, 6, 5 → 46 ; 5, 7, 6 → ?

**Options**

- **A.** 69
- **B.** 65
- **C.** 67
- **D.** 63

{{< details title="Show answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: C.** 67

Rule: (a + b) x c - a. Check: (3+5)x4 - 3 = 29. (4+6)x5 - 4 = 46. So (5+7)x6 - 5 = 72 - 5 = 67.
