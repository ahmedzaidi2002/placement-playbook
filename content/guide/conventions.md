---
title: Conventions
weight: 2
---

Each company page is built from a first-hand account written by a candidate. To keep the
line between what was reported and what was added later clear:

- **Reported** facts (numbers, formats, questions, what was said) come from the account and are
  stated plainly.
- **Editor's note** callouts hold anything added afterwards: a worked solution, a complexity
  table, a revision pointer. They are never presented as part of what the candidate said.
- **Not stated** is written wherever the account does not say (year, package, exact cut-offs),
  rather than guessing.
- Counts like "around 250 to 300 students" are the candidate's own estimates and are kept as
  estimates.
- Accounts are anonymous. The page names the campus or batch only when the account does.

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Hiring processes change year to year. Treat each company page as one data point about how that
company ran its process, not as a guarantee of the next one.
{{< /callout >}}
