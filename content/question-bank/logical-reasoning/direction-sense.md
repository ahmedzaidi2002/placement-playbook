### 1 · Q27-29, directions and relations

*Asked by: Accenture (On-campus recall)*

(27) A is east of B, B north of C, C west of D, D south of A. In which direction is C from A?
(28) A man walks 10 km south, turns left and walks 4 km, turns left and walks 4 km, turns
right and walks 4 km. Shortest distance from the start? [8 / 10 / 12 / 8.16] (29) Sinha points
to a man: "He is the brother of my uncle's daughter." How is the man related to Sinha?
[brother / brother-in-law / cousin / nephew]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (27) south-west (28) 10 km (29) cousin**

**Why.**
- (27) A east of B, B north of C, C west of D, D south of A: they form a rectangle with B top-left, A top-right, D bottom-right, C bottom-left. C is south-west of A.
- (28) 10 S; left (facing east) 4 E; left (facing north) 4 N; right (facing east) 4 E. Net: 8 east, 6 south. Distance √(8² + 6²) = 10 km.
- (29) The uncle's daughter's brother is the uncle's son: a cousin.

**Concept: track facing direction and use Pythagoras.** "Left" and "right" depend on the way you face, so update the heading at each turn. Add all east-west moves and all north-south moves separately; the straight-line distance is √(EW² + NS²).

### 2 · Q8, shadow before sunset

*Asked by: Axxela (NIT Bhopal 2024)*

One evening before sunset, Rajni and Sanjiv were talking face to face. If Sanjiv's shadow
was exactly to his right, which direction was Rajni facing?

- **(a)** North
- **(b)** North East
- **(c)** South
- **(d)** South East

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: (c) South**

**Why.** Before sunset the sun is in the west, so shadows fall to the east. Sanjiv's shadow is on his right, so east is on his right: he faces north. Rajni faces him, so she faces south.

**Concept: shadows and the sun.** Morning sun in the east → shadows point west; evening sun in the west → shadows point east. If you face north, east is on your right.
