### 1 · Q1-12, work, interest, averages, boats

*Asked by: Accenture (Aptitude set)*

(1) Some men finish a job in 45 days; with 10 fewer they take 60. How many men? [50/60/30/40]
(2) 5 men and 10 boys finish in 30 days; 8 men and 12 boys in 20. Ratio of a man's to a boy's
daily work? [5:1/4:5/6:1/7:3] (3) 4 women and 5 men do 3 times the work of 2 women and 1 man.
Man : woman? [1:1/3:2/1:2/2:1] (4) Manoj takes 20 days, Chandu 25; later Suresh joined and all
finished in 10 days; Rs 1000 is paid. Suresh's share? [100/300/200/400] (5) Rs 15{,}000 of
Rs 30{,}000 is lent at 6% simple interest; to earn 10% overall, what rate for the rest?
[8/12/14/16%] (6) Part of \$6600 earns 5% and the rest 3%; the 5% part earns twice the
other. Total income in a year? [270/250/280/200] (7) A weight of 63 kg was written as 83 kg,
raising the average by 0.5 kg. Group size? [25/20/40/60] (8) In a group of 8, two men aged 21
and 23 are replaced by two boys and the average rises by 2. Average age of the boys?
[17/30/28/23] (9) A boat takes 16 hours downstream from A to B and back to C between them;
still-water speed 9 km/h, stream 6 km/h. Distance A-C? [60/90/30/cannot be determined]
(10) Upstream takes 8 h 24 min for a distance; downstream takes 5 h for $5/7$ of it. Boat
: stream? [11:5/11:6/11:1/6:5] (11) 48 km downstream takes 128 min less than upstream;
stream 3 km/h. Boat speed? [12/15/6/9] (12) Alloys brass:iron:zinc $= 2:3:1$ and
iron:zinc:lead $= 5:4:3$ are melted in equal weights. Lead per kg of the new alloy?
[1/4 / 41/7 / 1/8 / 51/9]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (1) 40 (2) 6:1 (3) 1:1 (4) Rs 100 (5) 14% (6) 270 (7) 40 (8) 30 (9) cannot be determined (10) 11:1 (11) 12 km/h (12) 1/8**

**Why.**
1. Total work in man-days is fixed: 45x = 60(x − 10), so 15x = 600, x = 40 men.
2. Let a man do m and a boy b per day. 30(5m + 10b) = 1 and 20(8m + 12b) = 1, so 150m + 300b = 160m + 240b, giving 60b = 10m: m : b = 6 : 1.
3. 4w + 5m = 3(2w + m) gives 2m = 2w, so 1 : 1.
4. In 10 days Manoj does 10/20 = 50% and Chandu 10/25 = 40%, so Suresh did 10%: Rs 100.
5. 10% of 30,000 = 3000 needed. The first half earns 6% of 15,000 = 900, so the rest must earn 2100 on 15,000 = 14%.
6. Let x earn 5%: 0.05x = 2 × 0.03(6600 − x) → 0.11x = 396 → x = 3600. Income = 180 + 90 = 270.
7. The total rose by 83 − 63 = 20 kg and the average by 0.5, so n = 20/0.5 = 40.
8. The total rose by 8 × 2 = 16, so the boys total 21 + 23 + 16 = 60: average 30.
9. With C somewhere between A and B, the time equation has two unknowns (AB and BC), so it cannot be determined.
10. D = 8.4(S − R) and 5D/7 = 5(S + R), so D = 7(S + R). Then 8.4S − 8.4R = 7S + 7R → 1.4S = 15.4R → S : R = 11 : 1.
11. 48/(s − 3) − 48/(s + 3) = 128/60 → 288/(s² − 9) = 32/15 → s² − 9 = 135 → s = 12.
12. The first alloy has no lead; the second has 3/12 = 1/4 lead. Equal weights halve it: 1/8 kg per kg.

**Concepts used.**
- **Work = rate × time.** Treat a whole job as 1; a person who finishes in d days does 1/d per day. Rates add when people work together, and pay is shared in proportion to the work done.
- **Weighted average** (interest mixes, alloys): the overall rate is the size-weighted mean of the parts.
- **Average change**: change in total = change in average × count.
- **Boats and streams**: downstream speed = boat + stream, upstream = boat − stream; time = distance ÷ speed.

### 2 · Q13-25, mixtures, ages, simplification, series

*Asked by: Accenture (Aptitude set)*

(13) 6 L of free water is mixed with 20 L of milk costing Rs 28/L; all sold at cost price per
litre. Profit? [30 / $16\tfrac13$ / 25 / 16.5%] (14) 144 L of milk:water $= 5:7$; milk to add
for $23:21$? [40/28/32/36] (15) 30 kg at Rs 75 and 20 kg at Rs 70, sold at Rs 80. Gain?
[350/550/420/210] (16) Cost of 80 notebooks equals selling price of 65. Gain%? [32/42/27/23]
(17) Eight years ago Pranathi's age equalled her son's and daughter's present ages combined; in
5 years daughter : son $= 7:6$; her husband is 7 years older than her and three times the son.
Daughter's age? [19/27/15/23] (18) Married 8 years ago; now $9/7$ of her age then; her son is
$1/6$ of her present age. Son's age 3 years ago? [4/2/3/5] (19) Mani : Dheeraj $= x : 42$; Mani
is 8 younger than Murali, who will be 33 in 8 years; Dheeraj $-$ Mani $=$ Murali's age. $x$?
[18/10/16/17] (20) Revanth is 3 times his son and $4/5$ of his father; their average age is 62.
Father $-$ son? [64/69/66/62] (21) 36% of 945 $-$ 26% of 765 $+$ 17.7 [167/187/159/143]
(22) $\sqrt{456 \div 12 + 142 - 11}$ [11/169/23/13] (23) $1\tfrac15$ of $1\tfrac12$ of ? $= 216$
[100/125/140/120] (24) 15, 32, 60, 122, 240, ? [488/482/364/362] (25) 18, 10, 8, 9, 11.5, ?
[10.75/18.75/19.75/14.75]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (13) 30% (14) 32 (15) 350 (16) 23% (17) 23 (18) 3 (19) 17 (20) 66 (21) 159 (22) 13 (23) 120 (24) 482 (25) 14.75**

**Why.**
- (13) Cost = 20 × 28 = 560 for 26 L; sold at 28/L = 728. Profit 168/560 = 30%. Free water is pure profit.
- (14) Milk 60, water 84. (60 + x)/84 = 23/21 → 60 + x = 92 → x = 32.
- (15) Cost 30 × 75 + 20 × 70 = 3650; sale 50 × 80 = 4000; gain 350.
- (16) Cost of 80 = price of 65, so on 65 items you gain the cost of 15: 15/65 ≈ 23%.
- (17) Husband = 3 × son and = Pranathi + 7. Try son = 19: husband 57, Pranathi 50; 8 years ago she was 42 = son + daughter, so daughter 23. Check: (23 + 5) : (19 + 5) = 28 : 24 = 7 : 6.
- (18) x = (9/7)(x − 8) → x = 36; son = 36/6 = 6; three years ago, 3. (The key's 2 subtracts 4 years by mistake.)
- (19) Murali is 33 − 8 = 25, Mani 17, Dheeraj 17 + 25 = 42, so x = 17.
- (20) Revanth R, son R/3, father 5R/4. R + R/3 + 5R/4 = 186 → 31R/12 = 186 → R = 72. Father 90 − son 24 = 66.
- (21) 340.2 − 198.9 + 17.7 = 159. (22) √(38 + 142 − 11) = √169 = 13. (23) (6/5)(3/2)x = 216 → x = 120.
- (24) Alternate ×2 + 2 and ×2 − 4: 15→32→60→122→240→482.
- (25) Halve and add 1, 3, 5, 7, 9: 18→10→8→9→11.5→14.75.

**Concepts used.**
- **Profit on cost**: profit % = (SP − CP)/CP × 100. "Cost of a equals price of b" gives profit = (a − b)/b.
- **Mixtures**: only the added component changes; set up the new ratio and solve.
- **Ages**: write every person in terms of one unknown, then use the remaining fact to solve.
- **Series**: when differences are irregular, try "multiply then add" patterns or two interleaved rules.

### 3 · Q21-30, arithmetic and algebra

*Asked by: Accenture (Previous year)*

(21) If $f(x) = x^2 - 50$, $f(-5) = $ [75 / 25 / 0 / $-25$ / $-75$] (22) A helper makes 2 large
or 35 small cakes an hour; the kitchen is available 3 hours; 20 large and 700 small cakes are
needed. Helpers needed? [10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30] (23) $f(x) = (x+2)/(x-2)$; which is greatest?
[$f(-1)$ / $f(0)$ / $f(1)$ / $f(3)$ / $f(4)$] (24) If $p, q$ are perfect cubes, which is not
necessarily a cube? [$8p$ / $pq$ / $pq + 27$ / $-p$ / $(p - q)^6$] (25) Bows need 15 inches of
ribbon; how many from 4 yards? [8 / 9 / 10 / 11 / 12] (26) If $V = 12R/(r + R)$, $R = $
[$Vr/(12 - V)$ / ...] (27) Degrees moved by the hour hand from noon to 2:30 pm? [720 / 180 /
75 / 65 / 60] (28) $(3x + 2)(2x - 5) = ax^2 + kx + n$; $a - n + k = $ [5 / 8 / 9 / 10 / 11]
(29) Radius up 20%: area up by [44% / 120% / 144% / 40%] (30) Areas in ratio
$169 : 196$: radii in ratio [10:11 / 11:12 / 12:13 / 13:14]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (21) −25 (22) 10 (23) f(3) (24) pq + 27 (25) 9 (26) Vr/(12 − V) (27) 75 (28) 5 (29) 44% (30) 13:14**

**Why.**
- (21) f(−5) = 25 − 50 = −25 (the key's 25 is wrong).
- (22) Large cakes: 20/2 = 10 helper-hours; small: 700/35 = 20 helper-hours. 30 helper-hours in 3 hours needs 10 helpers.
- (23) f(−1) = −1/3, f(0) = −1, f(1) = −3, f(3) = 5, f(4) = 3: f(3) is greatest.
- (24) If p = a³ and q = b³: 8p = (2a)³, pq = (ab)³, −p = (−a)³, (p − q)⁶ = ((p − q)²)³ are all cubes; pq + 27 need not be (1 + 27 = 28).
- (25) 4 yards = 144 inches; 144/15 = 9.6, so 9 full bows.
- (26) V(r + R) = 12R → Vr = R(12 − V) → R = Vr/(12 − V).
- (27) The hour hand moves 30° per hour: 2.5 × 30 = 75°.
- (28) (3x + 2)(2x − 5) = 6x² − 11x − 10, so a − n + k = 6 + 10 − 11 = 5.
- (29) 1.2² = 1.44: up 44%. (30) Radii = √(169 : 196) = 13 : 14.

**Concepts used.**
- **Man-hours (here helper-hours)**: total work ÷ time available = number of workers.
- **Rearranging formulas**: multiply out the denominator, collect the wanted variable on one side, factor it out.
- **Area scales as the square of length**: so ratios of radii are square roots of area ratios.

### 4 · Q35-40, probability and arithmetic

*Asked by: Accenture (Previous year)*

(35) 4 boys and 3 girls sit in a row; probability they alternate? (36) Trains 2 km apart run
towards each other at 20 and 30 m/s; lengths 200 and 300 m. Time to cross? (37) Half a
distance at 40 km/h and half at 60 km/h: average speed? (38) A box holds 90 nuts of 100 g and
100 bolts of 150 g and weighs 35.5 kg; the empty box weighs [10 / 10.5 / 11 / 11.5 kg].
(39) A father is three times as old as his son; in 15 years he will be twice as old. Father's
age? [36 / 42 / 45 / 48] (40) Which is greatest? [40% of 30 / 3/5 of 25 / 6.5% of 200 / five
more than $3^2$ / an expression printed as "1/2-4"]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (35) 1/35 (36) 50 s (37) 48 km/h (38) 11.5 kg (39) 45 (40) the last option, if it reads 1/2⁻⁴ = 16**

**Why.**
- (35) Alternating means B G B G B G B. Boys in 4! ways, girls in 3! ways: 144 of 7! = 5040 arrangements, i.e. 1/35.
- (36) Closing speed 50 m/s. They must cover the 2000 m gap plus both lengths (500 m) to fully pass: 2500/50 = 50 s.
- (37) Equal distances at 40 and 60: average = 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 48 km/h.
- (38) Nuts 90 × 0.1 = 9 kg, bolts 100 × 0.15 = 15 kg; box = 35.5 − 24 = 11.5 kg.
- (39) F = 3S and F + 15 = 2(S + 15) → S = 15, F = 45.
- (40) 12, 15, 13 and 14 for the first four; the key picks the last, which fits only if it is 1/2⁻⁴ = 16.

**Concepts used.**
- **Average speed over equal distances is the harmonic mean** 2ab/(a + b), not (a + b)/2, because you spend longer at the slower speed.
- **Relative speed**: objects moving towards each other close the gap at the sum of their speeds.
- **Probability = favourable ÷ total** arrangements.
