### 1 · Q46-51, substituted operators

*Asked by: Accenture (IDC paper); Accenture (Dec paper)*

With `*<code> for &#36;&#92;div&#36;, </code>/<code> for &#36;-&#36;, </code>+<code> for &#36;&#92;times&#36;, </code>-` for $+$:
(46) $9/8*7+5-10 = ?$ [13.3 / 10.8 / 10.7 / 11.4] (47) $9/15*9+2-9 = ?$ [14.7 / 15.3 / 14.1 /
16.2] (48) Which is true? [$36/12*4+50-8 = -106$ / $12*8/4+50-8 = 45.5$ / $36*4/12+36-8 = 4.7$ /
$8*36/4+50-8 = 300$] (49) With A $= +$, S $= -$, M $= \times$, D $= \div$, V $= \,=$,
W $= >$, X $= <$, which is correct? [6 S 7 A 2 M 3 W 0 D 7 / 6 A 7 S 2 M 3 W 0 A 7 /
6 S 7 M 2 S 3 W 0 M 7 / 6 M 7 S 2 A 3 X 0 D 7] With `*<code> for &#36;-&#36;, </code>/` for $+$,
`+<code> for &#36;&#92;div&#36;, </code>-` for $\times$: (50) which is true? [$16/8*6+90-12 = 23.2$ /
$8*12/6+90-12 = 7.2$ / $16*6/8+16-12 = -4.1$ / $12*16/6+90-12 = 8$] (51) which is true?
[$16*4/18+16-8 = -10.1$ / $18*8/4+40-8 = -2.8$ / $16/18*4+40-8 = 33.2$ / $8*16/4+40-8 = -2$]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (46) 13.3 (47) 14.7 (48) the first (49) the first (50) the first (51) the third**

**Why.** Replace the symbols, then use normal order of operations (÷ and × before + and −).
- (46) 9 − 8 ÷ 7 × 5 + 10 = 9 − 5.71 + 10 = 13.29.
- (47) 9 − 15 ÷ 9 × 2 + 9 = 9 − 3.33 + 9 = 14.67.
- (48) 36 − 12 ÷ 4 × 50 + 8 = 36 − 150 + 8 = −106.
- (49) 6 − 7 + 2 × 3 = 5 > 0 ÷ 7 = 0, true.
- (50) 16 + 8 − 6 ÷ 90 × 12 = 24 − 0.8 = 23.2.
- (51) 16 + 18 − 4 ÷ 40 × 8 = 34 − 0.8 = 33.2.

**Concept: substitute first, then BODMAS.** Rewrite the whole expression with real operators before evaluating anything. Precedence follows the real operators (not the printed symbols): division and multiplication left to right, then addition and subtraction.

### 2 · Q45-46, substituted operators

*Asked by: Accenture (On-campus recall)*

(45) If $*$ means $+$, $+$ means $-$, $-$ means $\div$ and $/$ means $\times$:
$9 + 4 - 4 * 7 / 22 + 3 = $ [149 / 159 / 12 / 140] (46) If $+$ means $\times$, $-$ means
$\div$, $*$ means $-$ and $/$ means $+$: $4 * 15 - 3 / 9 + 2 = $ ?

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (45) 159 (46) 17**

**Why.**
- (45) 9 − 4 ÷ 4 + 7 × 22 − 3 = 9 − 1 + 154 − 3 = 159.
- (46) 4 − 15 ÷ 3 + 9 × 2 = 4 − 5 + 18 = 17.

**Concept: symbol substitution.** Write the translated expression on paper, then apply BODMAS. Mistakes come from evaluating while translating.
