### 1 · Q1-3, prepositions

*Asked by: Accenture (IDC paper)*

Fill the blanks. (1) I am writing to enquire ___ the possibility of hiring a conference
room. [of / about / into / after] (2) ___ having her lunch, she stood ___ the tree and
waited ___ him. [with, below, for / after, under, for / inside, further, to / about,
across, into] (3) ... fewer fish are making it to adulthood to be caught ___ trawlermen.
[in / into / by / with]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (1) about (2) After, under, for (3) by**

**Why.** (1) "Enquire about" something. (2) "After having her lunch", "stood under the tree", "waited for him". (3) "Caught by trawlermen": "by" introduces who does the action in a passive sentence.

**Concept: prepositions.** Time (after), place (under) and fixed verb pairs (wait for, enquire about). In the passive, the doer comes after "by".

### 2 · Q7-10, articles

*Asked by: Accenture (IDC paper)*

(7) ___ great writer is convinced that whatever he says is not an echo of what others have
said. [an / the / a / no article] (8) ___ Reserve Bank of India directed banks to closely
watch ___ spending through International Debit Cards. [a, the / the, the / the, a / ...]
(9) The officer received ___ official letter from ___ Ministry of IT in ___ Central
Government. [a, the, an / a, an, the / an, the, the / an, an, the] (10) You cannot send out
___ uneducated man into ___ world of technology. [an, an / a, an / an, the / the, an]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (7) A (8) The, the (9) An, the, the (10) An, the**

**Why.**
- (7) "A great writer" = any great writer (general statement).
- (8) "The Reserve Bank of India" (a unique institution); "the spending" (specific spending through cards).
- (9) "An official letter" (vowel sound), "the Ministry of IT", "the Central Government" (specific bodies).
- (10) "An uneducated man" (vowel sound), "the world of technology" (specific).

**Concept: articles.** "A/an" = one of many, mentioned for the first time; "an" before a vowel **sound** (an hour, a university). "The" = a specific or unique thing both speakers know.

### 3 · Q1-10, grammar

*Asked by: Accenture (Dec paper)*

(1) She has great love and affection ___ her grandmother. [for / from / with / to]
(2) There is more ___ Argentina's red wines than Malbec. [in / to / from / at]
(3) Opaque glass is the material ___ which you cannot see. [into / through / from / between]
Nearest meaning: (4) 25-paise coins are becoming *obsolete* [smaller / older / rare /
outdated]; (5) a piece of *crumpled* paper [torn / burnt / wrinkled / dirty]; (6) his
plans went *awry* [well / smoothly / wrong / slowly]. Articles (a / an / the / none):
(7) The Brahmaputra rises in the Himalayas in ___ Tibet. (8) It is suspected that ___
colleague committed the murder. (9) ___ ice-pick was used for the murder. (10) It was no
doubt ___ stupidity to admit her into our gang.

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (1) for (2) to (3) through (4) outdated (5) wrinkled (6) wrong (7) no article (8) a (9) An (10) a** (the key's choice; "no article" is also defensible)

**Why.**
- (1) Love and affection **for** someone. (2) "There is more **to** X than Y" = X has more aspects. (3) You see **through** glass.
- (4) Obsolete = no longer used, outdated. (5) Crumpled = crushed into wrinkles. (6) Awry = gone wrong.
- (7) Country names usually take no article (Tibet). (8) "A colleague" = one unspecified colleague. (9) "An ice-pick" (vowel sound, first mention).
- (10) "It was a stupidity" treats one foolish act as countable; "stupidity" is usually uncountable, so "no article" is defensible.

**Concept: articles with countable vs uncountable nouns.** Countable singular nouns need an article; uncountable nouns (water, stupidity, advice) usually don't, unless you mean one instance.

### 4 · Q1-10, grammar

*Asked by: Accenture (Previous year)*

(1) This train travels from London ___ Paris. [at / to / over / below] (2) We stood at the
back ___ the theatre. [of / on / in / for] (3) I will work ___ five o'clock. [until /
up / in / to] Nearest meaning: (4) the *antidote* to these problems [cause for / result
of / remedy for / consequence of]; (5) a family *feud* [crisis / trouble / problem /
quarrel]; (6) a *lucid* style [elaborate / clear / intricate / noble]. Articles:
(7) ___ man ran into the street. A car hit ___ man. (8) The interesting thing about
___ Romans is the roads they built. (9) Einstein was ___ famous scientist. He won ___
Nobel Prize in 1921 ... and lived in ___ United States. (10) Are you shopping for ___
health club? ... choosing ___ wrong club.

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (1) to (2) of (3) until (4) remedy for (5) quarrel (6) clear (7) A, the (8) the (9) a, the, the (10) a, the**

**Why.**
- (1) From London **to** Paris. (2) At the back **of** the theatre. (3) Work **until** five (up to that time).
- (4) Antidote = remedy. (5) Feud = long quarrel. (6) Lucid = clear.
- (7) "A man" (first mention), "the man" (now known). (8) "The Romans" (a specific people). (9) "a famous scientist", "the Nobel Prize", "the United States". (10) "a health club" (any), "the wrong club" (a specific one).

**Concept: first mention vs known thing.** "A" introduces something new; "the" refers back to it or to something unique.
