### 1 · Q4-6, synonyms

*Asked by: Accenture (IDC paper)*

Nearest meaning of the italicised word. (4) The jacket is *impervious* to water.
[dirty / pure / impenetrable / favorable] (5) Chandan was *chagrined* with the continuous
disruption of power. [delighted / creation / peeved / security] (6) The latest
*ordinance* issued by the government ... [decision / law / opinion / verdict]

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answers: (4) impenetrable (5) peeved (6) law**

**Why.** Impervious = not letting anything through. Chagrined = annoyed or embarrassed (peeved). An ordinance is an executive decree with the force of law.

**Concept: synonyms in context.** Replace the word with each option and keep the one that preserves the sentence's meaning.

### 2 · Q27, synonym of candid

*Asked by: EPAM (EPAM freshers test)*

Find the synonym of the word "candid".

- **(a)** Biased
- **(b)** Candy
- **(c)** Sweet
- **(d)** Sincere

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: (d) sincere**

**Why.** Candid means frank and honest. "Candy" is a sound-alike trap.

**Concept: avoid sound-alike traps.** Similar spelling does not mean similar meaning.

### 3 · synonym of dignity

*Asked by: Flipkart (Neev 2025)*

What is the synonym for the word "dignity"?

- **(a)** Poise
- **(b)** Immorality
- **(c)** Evil
- **(d)** Indecency

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: (a) poise**

**Why.** Dignity is calm self-respect; poise is calm, composed self-assurance. The others are negative.

**Concept: synonyms share tone.** A positive word's synonym is also positive; eliminate negative options first.

### 4 · antonym of persuasion

*Asked by: Flipkart (Neev 2025)*

What is the antonym for the word "persuasion"?

- **(a)** Dissuasion
- **(b)** Alluring
- **(c)** Convince
- **(d)** Cajole

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: (a) dissuasion**

**Why.** Persuasion convinces someone to do something; dissuasion convinces them not to. Cajole and convince are near-synonyms of persuade.

**Concept: prefixes for opposites.** dis-, un-, in-, non- often form antonyms: persuade/dissuade, agree/disagree.
