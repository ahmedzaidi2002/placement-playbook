### 1 · Q11, analogy

*Asked by: ION Group (SDE OA, Jul 2025)*

BUTTER is to BREAD as

- **(a)** jam is to jelly
- **(b)** paint is to wood
- **(c)** toast is to jelly
- **(d)** head is to foot
- **(e)** yellow is to blue

{{< details title="Answer and explanation" closed="true" >}}

**Answer: (b) paint is to wood**

**Why.** Butter is spread on bread to coat it, as paint is spread on wood.

**Concept: analogies.** Make a sentence for the first pair ("X is spread on Y") and test each option with the same sentence.
