This book is the theory of data structures and algorithms that a placement interview or an online assessment asks about: what each structure costs and why, every standard sorting algorithm with its exact properties, why a hash table is $O(1)$ and when it is not, and the standard algorithms on trees and graphs with their complexities. It is not a problem-solving sheet; it is what you need to answer “why?” three times in a row. Every trace in the book (an array after $k$ passes of a sort, a heap after a sequence of inserts, a hash table after a sequence of keys, a Dijkstra table) was produced by a real program, and the box under each listing is its real output.
